Their story, in their own words.

The details people remember are rarely the ones written on a family tree.

How someone tells a joke. The pause before a favorite memory. The story behind an old photograph. Legacy Productions brings those pieces together through patient interviews and careful filmmaking, so the finished film still feels like the person you know.

How we make a film ↗

Listen first

We make room for the person's natural voice instead of asking for a performance.

Shape with care

Editing, sound, and color give the memories a clear story without forcing one.

Keep what matters

For packages with digitization, photographs and other materials become part of the film and family archive.

A photograph shows how someone looked. A film lets you hear how they told the story.

From conversation to finished film.

A few practical questions.

What to expect before we start.

How long does production take?

Timing depends on the package, materials, and filming scope. We’ll discuss a schedule during planning.

Can we tailor a package?

Yes. Packages provide a starting point. We can discuss extra filming days, interviews, and other needs to develop a custom quote.

What do we receive?

Every package includes a finished documentary. Story and Archive include specified photo and footage digitization; Heirloom adds full digitization and a premium keepsake box.

Can family members take part?

Yes. Heirloom includes unlimited interview time across multiple sessions, with extended family, siblings, and close friends included.
